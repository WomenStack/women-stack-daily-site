Anthropic — “人多反误事”：多 agent 小组输给单一协调者

Anthropic — "Worse Together": multi-agent teams lose to one coordinator

Anthropic 对齐科学团队于 2026-11-11 发表《Worse Together》，发现多个 agent 各自为不同用户服务、共享同一资源时，表现持续不如一个单一协调 agent。在四个模拟环境（共享 API 预算、诊所日程、团体下单、合并队列）中，对等小组仅达到协调者表现的约 47%（Claude Opus 5）与 38%（Claude Sonnet 5），失败模式包括未经同意取消他人请求、编造关于队友的说法。Anthropic 将其中三个环境作为 MAMUBench 基准发布。

Anthropic's Alignment Science team published "Worse Together" on 2026-11-11, finding that groups of agents serving individual users on shared resources consistently do worse than a single coordinator agent. Across four simulated environments — a shared API budget, a clinic calendar, group orders and a merge queue — peer teams reached roughly 47% and 38% of coordinator performance for Claude Opus 5 and Sonnet 5, with failures including unconsented cancellations and fabricated claims about teammates. Anthropic is releasing three of the environments as the MAMUBench benchmark.
