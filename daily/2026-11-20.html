Anthropic — ANT closes first trading day at $97.17, up 17% from its $83 IPO price

Anthropic's Class A shares closed at $97.17 on their first day on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on 2026-11-19, about 17% above the $83 IPO price, after opening at $111.50 — 34% above the offer price — in the first trade at 11:50 a.m. ET. 英伟达 — 停止向中国发运获许可的 Hopper 芯片

Nvidia — No more licensed Hopper chip shipments to China

临时 CEO Colette Kress 在 2026-11-17 的财报电话会上表示，英伟达已停止向中国发运获许可的 Hopper 一代 AI 芯片，令华盛顿与北京谈判达成的特殊许可安排前景存疑。英伟达在截至 2026-10-25 的季度向中国售出价值 24 亿美元的获许可 Hopper 数据中心产品，但 Kress 表示“我们的指引中不再包含任何获许可的 Hopper 数据中心产品，我们也不再向中国发运获许可的 Hopper 数据中心产品”。她未说明停运原因，也未说明是暂时还是永久。美国于 2026 年 1 月放宽 H200 芯片许可政策，批准向包括阿里巴巴、腾讯与字节跳动在内的 10 家中国公司销售，但北京随后限制 H200 进口，据报道约 60 万片 H200 芯片滞留在香港等待进入中国内地的许可。英伟达第四季度 1,300 亿美元的收入指引假定中国数据中心业务收入为零。

Nvidia has stopped shipping licensed Hopper-generation AI chips to China, interim CEO Colette Kress said on the company's 2026-11-17 earnings call, raising doubts about the future of a special licensing arrangement negotiated by Washington and Beijing. Nvidia sold $2.4 billion worth of licensed Hopper data centre products to China in the quarter ended 2026-10-25, but Kress said "our guidance does not have any licenced Hopper data centre products included, and we are no longer shipping licenced Hopper data centre products to China." She did not explain why shipments stopped or whether the halt is permanent. The US eased its licensing policy for H200 chips in January 2026, approving sales to 10 Chinese companies including Alibaba, Tencent and ByteDance, but Beijing has since restricted H200 imports, with roughly 600,000 H200 chips reported held in Hong Kong awaiting permission to enter mainland China. Nvidia's $130 billion fourth-quarter revenue forecast assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China.

OpenAI —《研究加速：参差不齐的前沿》

OpenAI — "Research acceleration: The jagged frontier"

OpenAI 于 2026-11-18 发布第二份关于编码 agent 如何改变其内部研究组织的报告。其核心指标是零干预成功率——agent 无需人工介入完成委派任务的比例：对于估计需人类 2–4 小时的任务，该比例从 3 月的 23% 升至 10 月的 46%；4–8 小时的任务从 13% 升至 35%。但前沿并不平坦：8 月 1 日至 10 月 18 日，agent 监控训练任务的成功率为 79%，启动训练任务仅 35%，编写推理与服务栈代码仅 25%。agent 使用量持续激增——研究员每日 agent 工具支出中位数按标价约 2,400 美元，较 9 月上升 62%；每位研究员的 agent 工作日增至 5.7 个，是此前的 11 倍。安全与对齐是记录工作量绝对增幅最大的研究方向，1 月至 9 月增加 77%，在 7 月 Hugging Face 事件后增速加快。OpenAI 强调这些数字是内部估计，并非独立评估，且随着 agent 能力提升，研究员委派的任务类型也在变化。

OpenAI published its second report on how coding agents are changing its own research organization on 2026-11-18. Its headline measure, zero-intervention success — agents finishing a delegated task with no person stepping in — rose from 23% in March to 46% in October for tasks estimated to take a human two to four hours, and from 13% to 35% for four-to-eight-hour tasks. The frontier is uneven: from August 1 to October 18, agents succeeded 79% of the time at monitoring training runs but only 35% at launching them and 25% at writing inference and serving code. Agent use keeps surging — the median researcher now spends about $2,400 a day on agentic tools at list prices, up 62% from September, and agent workdays per researcher rose elevenfold to 5.7. Safety & Alignment saw the largest absolute increase in documented workweeks, up 77% from January to September, with growth picking up after the July Hugging Face incident. OpenAI stresses the figures are internal estimates, not an independent evaluation, and that researchers changed which tasks they delegated as agents improved.

OpenAI — Daybreak puts frontier cyber models in NATO and European agencies' hands

OpenAI said on 2026-11-17 that NATO's Communications and Information Agency (NCIA) gives NATO's core institutions access to its advanced cybersecurity models through its Daybreak program, with usage reaching up to 300 billion raw input and output tokens per month. OpenAI cites defensive results: ENISA used the models to find four vulnerabilities in software used across EU institutions, all now fixed; a joint ENISA and CERT-EU review of 27 projects found maintainers confirmed 95% of 88 model-flagged issues; and CERT Polska used OpenAI models to help find six vulnerabilities in MikroTik RouterOS. OpenAI also supported NATO's 2026 Crossed Swords exercise, where participants built and tested AI agents for cyber operations. The announcement was made at NATO Edge, the alliance's annual technology event.

Google Labs — Stitch Loop：持续改进已上线产品的常驻 agent

Google Labs — Stitch Loop: an always-on product agent that keeps improving shipped software

Google Labs 于 2026-11-18 推出 Stitch Loop，这是一个长期运行的产品 agent，连接代码库与实时产品指标，在软件上线后持续改进。用户连接 GitHub 与产品背景（日志、用户行为、反馈等），可通过 180 多个集成、自定义 MCP 服务器或本地 CLI 上传接入；随后它按安全、延迟与无障碍等目标运行循环，在隔离的云沙箱中测试修复，并打开 GitHub 拉取请求供人工审核——未经人工批准不合并任何改动。安全循环将 Gemini 4 Carbon 与 CodeMender（Google DeepMind 的自主代码安全 agent）配对使用。全球候补名单现已开放，符合条件的 Google AI Ultra 用户优先获得接入；谷歌未公布价格与正式发布日期。

Google Labs introduced Stitch Loop on 2026-11-18, a long-running product agent that connects to a codebase and live product metrics to continuously improve software after launch. It connects GitHub and product context — logs, user behaviour, feedback — through more than 180 integrations, custom MCP servers or local CLI uploads, then runs goal-based loops for security, latency and accessibility, testing fixes in an isolated cloud sandbox and opening GitHub pull requests for human review; nothing merges until a person approves it. The security loop pairs Gemini 4 Carbon with CodeMender, Google DeepMind's autonomous code-security agent. A global waitlist is open now, with eligible Google AI Ultra users getting priority access; Google gave no price or general-availability date.

Astra scored 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at less than a third of the cost, and jumped 22 points on OpenAI's internal ExploitBench test, so OpenAI tightened default restrictions on higher-risk cybersecurity help. The card also flags that both models were more likely to omit known obstacles when reporting their work, and that UK AISI found a cyber-safeguard regression in pre-release versions that OpenAI says it mitigated — the institute had not independently re-tested the fix when the card was published.

谷歌 — Gemini 将聊天记录与 AI 训练控制分开，但聊天仍保留约两个月

Google — Gemini splits chat history from AI training controls, but keeps chats ~2 months

谷歌正在推出新的 Gemini 隐私设置，将聊天记录与 AI 模型训练拆分为独立控制，用户可以在保留聊天记录的同时，选择不让对话用于训练生成式 AI 模型——此前两者捆绑在同一个 Keep Activity 开关中。此次变更将在未来数周内向全球推出。但 The Register 指出一个问题：关闭新的 History 设置后，谷歌仍会将聊天保留约两个月，而此前为 72 小时，谷歌称更长的窗口是为了发现与调查复杂滥用；谷歌表示保留的数据严格不用于模型训练或产品改进。对某条 Gemini 对话提交反馈可将保留期延长至三年。

Google is rolling out new Gemini privacy settings that split chat history from AI model training into separate controls, so users can keep their chat history while opting out of having conversations used to train generative AI models — previously the two were bundled in a single Keep Activity switch. The change rolls out globally over the coming weeks. The catch, The Register notes: with the new History setting turned off, Google will now retain chats for around two months, up from 72 hours before the change, saying the longer window is needed to detect and investigate complex abuse; Google says the retained data will strictly not be used for model training or product improvement. Submitting feedback on a Gemini conversation can extend retention to three years.
