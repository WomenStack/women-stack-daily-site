OpenAI — ChatGPT Wallet 让 dots agent 替用户付款

OpenAI — ChatGPT Wallet lets dots agents pay for users

OpenAI 于 2026-11-13 推出 ChatGPT Wallet，让 dots agent 可以替用户完成购买。钱包与 American Express、Mastercard、Visa 合作，卡片只需保存一次，之后发出支付令牌，agent 永远看不到卡号；每笔购买都需要用户确认并通过生物识别验证。首批仅限美国 18 岁及以上的 ChatGPT Pro 订阅用户，且须持有符合条件的美国发行的卡片；OpenAI 表示上线初期不从交易中抽成。

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on 2026-11-13, letting its dots agents complete purchases on a user's behalf. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, the wallet stores the card once and issues payment tokens, so the agent never sees the card number; every purchase needs the user's review and a biometric check. At launch it is limited to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over with eligible US-issued cards, and OpenAI says it takes no cut of transactions at launch.

Google — Gemini 4 Carbon 登顶 MathArena，但成本是 GPT-6.1 Sol 的 15 倍

Google — Gemini 4 Carbon tops MathArena, at 15x the cost of GPT-6.1 Sol

Google 的 Gemini 4 Carbon 在独立数学基准 MathArena 上以 85.4% 的预期表现登顶，领先 Anthropic 的 Claude Fable 5.2（82.8%）与 OpenAI 的 GPT-6.1 Sol（80.6%）——这是好一阵子以来首次由非 OpenAI 模型领跑该榜。代价是成本：按测试所用的推理设置，Carbon 每道题约 15.70 美元，Sol 只要 1.07 美元，相差约 15 倍，Fable 5.2 则要 40.20 美元。三者的误差范围有重叠，Carbon 最常见的失败原因是超出时限。

Google's Gemini 4 Carbon took first place on MathArena, the independent maths benchmark, with 85.4% expected performance, ahead of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 at 82.8% and OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol at 80.6% — the first time in a while a non-OpenAI model has led the board. The catch is cost: at the reasoning settings used in testing, a Carbon run costs about $15.70 per problem against $1.07 for Sol, roughly 15 times more, and Fable 5.2 costs $40.20. The three scores' error ranges overlap, and Carbon's most common failure mode was exceeding the time limit.
