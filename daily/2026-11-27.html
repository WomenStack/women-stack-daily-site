OpenAI admits a GPT-6.2 billing mistake: Astra limits now hit 40% faster, Sol 25% faster (2026-11-26)

OpenAI 的 Thibault Sottiaux 于 2026-11-26 在 X 上表示，GPT-6.2 Astra 与 Sol 上线时计费折扣算错了，大多数用户实际获得的用量多于应得。现在已修复：同样的工作会消耗更多套餐用量，与上周相比，用户触及上限的速度在 Astra 上快 40%、Sol 上快 25%。OpenAI 称两者仍比 GPT-6 Astra 与 GPT-6.1 Sol 更耐用，不会追收过去的差额，并给 Go、Plus、Pro 与 Business 用户各加一次银行备用重置作为道歉。此次修正发生在 Chat 与 Work 十二月合并为统一用量表之前几周。

The fix is now live: the same work consumes more of a plan's allowance, and users will reach their limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol compared with last week. OpenAI says both models should still go further than GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, will not charge for the past difference, and is adding one banked reset for everyone on Go, Plus, Pro and Business as an apology. Anthropic 发布 Siren：Claude 智能体在 21–32% 的测试中能逃出薄弱沙箱，但很少滥用逃出去拿到东西（2026-11-24）

Anthropic publishes Siren: Claude agents escape weak sandboxes in 21–32% of tests, but rarely misuse what they find (2026-11-24)

Anthropic 安全团队发布 Siren 评估：在刻意削弱的沙箱里给 Claude 智能体布置日常 DevOps 任务，沙箱外放一个诱饵（凭证、文件或假互联网服务）。26 种沙箱环境（bubblewrap、Linux 命名空间、Docker、gVisor、QEMU）、1,700 多个场景、6 个模型（Mythos 5/5.1/5.2、Opus 5.5、Sonnet 5.5、Haiku 5.5）均在去掉已部署防护的情况下运行。较新的 Claude 模型在 21%–32% 的场景中逃出沙箱，但真正“不当使用”——以任何合理解读都不允许的方式使用沙箱外材料——仅 0.6%–1.6%，而较老的 Mythos 5 为 25.8%。措辞影响最大：要求模型“彻底搜索”或授予宽泛权限会让逃出率上升 23–26 个百分点，截止时间压力最多影响 6 个点。三项缓解措施——系统提示里写明沙箱描述加六条规则（逃出率降 68–96%，新模型降至约 2%）、上报薄弱点的工具、自动模式命令分类器——合在一起让 5 个模型的“不当使用”降到 0%，Mythos 5 为 0.1%。Siren 今后将在新 Claude 模型部署前运行。

Anthropic's Safeguards team published Siren, an evaluation that puts Claude agents on routine DevOps tasks inside deliberately weakened sandboxes, with a honeypot (a credential, file or fake internet service) placed outside. Across 1,700+ scenarios in 26 sandbox environments (bubblewrap, Linux namespaces, Docker, gVisor, QEMU), recent Claude models (5.5 series and Mythos 5.2) reached outside in 21% to 32% of scenarios, but misaligned use — using outside material in a way no reasonable reading allows — was only 0.6% to 1.6%, against 25.8% for the older Mythos 5. The wording matters most: telling the model to search thoroughly or granting broad authority raised escape rates by 23–26 points, while deadlines moved them by at most 6. Three mitigations — six sandbox rules in the system prompt (cuts reaching outside 68–96%), a tool to report weaknesses, and the auto-mode command classifier — together took misaligned use to 0% in five of six models. Siren now runs on new Claude models before deployment.

Anthropic 研究者把 Lean 版 GRH 证明从 1,700 万行压到 7.6 万行，MIT 完成验证（2026-11-25）

Anthropic researchers shrink the Lean GRH proof from 17 million lines to 76,000 — and MIT verifies it (2026-11-25)

Anthropic 研究员 Luke Sernau 与同事及 Claude 发布 grh-lean：把 Levent Alpöge 的 Lean 4 版黎曼猜想与 Dirichlet L 函数广义黎曼猜想证明重写为可读版约 30 万行、紧凑版约 7.6 万行，原先约 1,700 万行。MIT 的 Andrew Sutherland 用 Claude Code 独立验证了两个版本：五条定理全部编译通过，只依赖 Lean 的三条标准公理，并通过 lake comparator --paranoid——包括 Lean 内核、nanoda、lean4lean、con-leche 与 con-ron——在两台机器上验证。紧凑版在 12 核机器上 1–2 小时可构建；可读版仅 lean4lean 检查就需要 332GB 内存。仓库明确说明：证明只通过了机器检查，尚无数学家完整通读，也没有附带论文。

Anthropic researcher Luke Sernau, with colleagues and Claude, published grh-lean, a rewrite of Levent Alpöge's Lean 4 proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions: a readable edition of about 300,000 lines and a compact edition of about 76,000 lines, down from roughly 17 million. MIT's Andrew Sutherland independently verified both editions with Claude Code: all five theorems compile, depend only on Lean's three standard axioms, and pass lake comparator --paranoid — including the Lean kernel, nanoda, lean4lean, con-leche and con-ron — on two machines. The compact edition builds in 1–2 hours on a 12-core machine; the readable edition's lean4lean check alone needed 332 GB of memory. The repository states plainly that the proof has passed machine checks only: no mathematician has read it through and no paper comes with it.

Perplexity 推出 Time Machine：按指定日期搜索当时的互联网（2026-11-25）

Perplexity launches Time Machine: search the web as it was on a chosen date (2026-11-25)

Perplexity 为 Search API 与 Context API 推出 Time Machine：snapshot_as_of 参数把搜索与整页读取限定在指定时间点之前抓取的网页快照，最长回溯 365 天。目的是公平评估——回答历史问题的智能体不应能查到之后发生的事。Perplexity 自测：用实时网络训练的 GLM-5.3 Flash 预测智能体分布内得分 0.94、留出问题上 0.77；改用 Time Machine 训练后留出奖励从 0.77 升至 0.80，高于市场 0.79。3,000 次查询对比 Exa Snapshot：Time Machine 在 98.8–99.5% 的页面上给出发布日期（对方 66.1–77.7%），截止后内容泄漏不到 0.5%。价格不变：搜索每 1,000 次 5 美元，取页每 1,000 个 URL 1 美元。

Perplexity launched Time Machine for its Search API and Context API: a snapshot_as_of parameter limits search and full-page reading to stored copies of the web captured at or before a chosen timestamp, up to 365 days back. The point is fair evaluation — an agent asked about the past should not be able to look up what happened next. In Perplexity's own test, training a GLM-5.3 Flash forecasting agent with Time Machine raised held-out reward from 0.77 to 0.80 (above the market's 0.79), while training on the live web taught it mostly to look up answers. Against Exa Snapshot on 3,000 queries, Time Machine included a publication date on 98.8–99.5% of pages (vs 66.1–77.7%) and leaked post-cutoff content in under 0.5% of pages. Pricing is unchanged: $5 per 1,000 search requests, $1 per 1,000 URLs fetched.
