OpenAI——ChatGPT Wallet 让 agent 代为付款

OpenAI — ChatGPT Wallet lets agents pay for purchases

OpenAI 于 2026-11-13 推出 ChatGPT Wallet，让 dots agent 在不接触用户卡号的情况下代为付款，每笔交易都需要一次安全验证。与 American Express、Mastercard、Visa 合作打造，初期仅面向美国 18 岁以上、持有合格美国发卡行卡片的 ChatGPT Pro 订阅用户。每次购买前，用户先核对商品、总额与卡片，再用 Face ID 或指纹批准；支付网络随后签发一次性 token。OpenAI 上线时不从交易中抽成，并同步发布了面向商家的《Commerce Partner Playbook》。

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on 2026-11-13, letting its dots agents pay for purchases without accessing users' card details, with a security check required for every transaction. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, it is initially available in the US to ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older with eligible US-issued cards. Users review items, total and card before each purchase and approve with Face ID or a fingerprint; the payment network then issues a single-use token. OpenAI takes no cut of transactions at launch and published a Commerce Partner Playbook for merchants.
