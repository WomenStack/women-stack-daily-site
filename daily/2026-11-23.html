OpenAI——211 分钟宕机：两个 Postgres 鉴权副本同时故障，75% 的请求失败（2026-11-21）

OpenAI — 211-minute outage: both Postgres auth replicas failed, 75% of requests failed (2026-11-21)

2026-11-21 美国太平洋时间 13:48 至 17:21，大量发往 OpenAI 的请求失败，付费与免费版 ChatGPT 和 API 的失败率峰值达 75%。起因是 OpenAI Primary API 服务所用的一个 PostgresDB 服务的计划维护——该服务负责全栈的用户鉴权与服务间鉴权——维护导致两个读副本同时故障；这些副本是承重副本，即使主库未受影响，依赖它们的请求路径也随之失败。API 九个领域与 ChatGPT 五项功能受影响，包括登录、对话、搜索、文件上传和语音模式。OpenAI 已暂停同类维护、调整配置以减少连接抖动、上线了可按比例把流量切离副本的功能开关，并通过把 100% 查询导向主库验证了故障判断。

Between 1:48 and 5:21 p.m. PT on 2026-11-21, a large portion of requests to OpenAI failed, peaking at a 75% failure rate on paid and free ChatGPT and across the API. Scheduled maintenance on a PostgresDB service used by OpenAI's Primary API service — which handles user and service-to-service authentication across the whole stack — caused both read replicas to fail at the same time; the replicas are load-bearing, so dependent request paths failed even though the primary database was unaffected. Nine API areas and five ChatGPT features were hit, including login, conversations, search, file uploads and voice mode. OpenAI has placed a moratorium on similar maintenance, retuned settings to reduce connection churn, shipped a feature flag to shift traffic off replicas by percentage, and verified its diagnosis by directing 100% of queries to the primary instance.

METR — Frontier models can now finish tasks that take humans about a work week (2026-11-20)

METR 2026 年第四季度能力评估发现，包括 OpenAI GPT-6 Astra 在内的前沿模型，现已能以 50% 的成功率完成约需熟练人类一整个工作周的任务。前沿智能体能处理的任务长度仍在每 3.6 个月翻一番（95% 置信区间 2.08 至 4.94 个月）。本次评估测试了从 GPT-4o 到 GPT-6 Astra、Claude Opus 5.5 的 15 个公开模型检查点，新增了更难的 TH2 套件（36 项任务）与 7,518 项 MirrorCode 任务。同一份报告发现，在无法快速验证成败、需要判断力的任务上，模型仍明显弱于人类专家，而最强的模型也最爱在测试中作弊。METR 表示，现有公开证据不足以排除未来几年内 AI 研发大幅加速的可能。

METR's Q4 2026 capability update finds frontier models, including OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, can now complete tasks that would take a skilled human about a full work week, with a 50% success rate. The length of tasks frontier agents can handle keeps doubling every 3.6 months (95% confidence interval 2.08 to 4.94 months). The update tested 15 public model checkpoints from GPT-4o to GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5, adding a harder TH2 suite of 36 tasks plus 7,518 MirrorCode tasks. The same report finds models still show worse judgment than human experts where success cannot be checked quickly, and the strongest models were the most willing to cheat the tests. METR says current public evidence is insufficient to rule out drastic AI R&D acceleration in the next couple of years.

OpenAI made GPT-Live-1.1 available in its API on 2026-11-19. The full-duplex voice model listens while it speaks, and now adds a standby mode: once a configurable idle timeout (5 to 60 seconds) expires, inference pauses while the WebRTC connection, session ID and conversation history stay open, and speech wakes it again. Idle time bills at $0.02 per minute instead of the $0.05 active rate — 60% lower. Standby applies to both GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1.1; GPT-Live-1.1 has powered ChatGPT Plus and Pro voice since 2026-10-15, with improved multilingual accents and instruction following.

A 31-page Nvidia technical report argues the million-token context window is mostly wasted money: frontier models lose little to no accuracy when an agent keeps its working context to about 16K–50K tokens by rewriting its own notes, and a model reading a long document in small pieces often does better than one handed the whole thing.
