Lean / Mathlib — 黎曼证明获认证：“我们完全有信心”（2026-11-21）

Lean / Mathlib — Riemann proof certified: "We have full confidence" (2026-11-21)

Lean 与 Mathlib 于 2026-11-21 发布联合声明，认证 Levent Alpöge 的黎曼猜想证明：“我们完全有信心，Alpöge 的黎曼猜想证明是正确的。”该陈述采用 Mathlib 自身的 RiemannHypothesis，由 Lean 与数论专家仔细核查；1740 万行 Lean 形式化代码可以编译，证明已被 Lean 内核、lake comparator 以及 con-leche、con-ron 等独立核查器接受，数十人已独立编译。Scientific American 报道，独立数学家认为该证明“几乎肯定正确”；《华尔街日报》的相关报道因付费墙未打开。

Lean and Mathlib published a joint statement on 2026-11-21 certifying Levent Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis: "We have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is correct." The statement is Mathlib's own RiemannHypothesis, vetted by experts in Lean and number theory; the 17-million-line Lean formalisation compiles, and the proof has been accepted by the Lean kernel, the lake comparator, and independent checkers including con-leche and con-ron, with dozens of people compiling it independently. Scientific American reports independent mathematicians attest the proof is "almost certainly true"; the Wall Street Journal's piece on the claim is paywalled and was not opened.

OpenAI — 对抗对抗性蒸馏（2026-11-20）

OpenAI — Combatting adversarial distillation (2026-11-20)

OpenAI 介绍 GPT-6.2 如何把受保护的推理与对话历史绑定，使窃取的模型行为无法在新的对话中重放：当攻击者复用此前的请求时，提取成功率从 54.0% 降至 0.2%；在当前对话中则从 22.9% 降至 0.9%，测试针对自适应黑盒攻击者与六个攻击模型。文章点名 DeepSeek（今年 2 月向国会通报时被提及）与 Moonshot AI（9 月的一场行动）；OpenAI 称每周对数千至数万个账号采取执法行动，并支持《芯片安全法》《AI OVERWATCH 法案》与《MATCH 法案》。

OpenAI describes how GPT-6.2 ties protected reasoning to conversation history so that extracted model behaviour cannot be replayed in a new conversation: extraction success fell from 54.0% to 0.2% when the attacker reuses an earlier request, and from 22.9% to 0.9% in the current conversation, against an adaptive black-box attacker and six attacker models. The post names DeepSeek, flagged in a February briefing to Congress, and Moonshot AI, behind a September campaign, as distillation offenders; OpenAI says it enforces against thousands to tens of thousands of accounts a week, and supports the Chip Security Act, the AI OVERWATCH Act and the MATCH Act.

DeepMind Institute — "What comes after AGI?" (2026-11-19)

Tim Genewein 探讨通用人工智能之后会发生什么，提出四条路径——规模化、范式转变、agent 协作与递归自我改进。有效算力每年至少增长 10 倍，他写道，10 亿美元的认知工作量十年内可能只需 1000 美元，agent 数量到 2030 年可能增长 1000 倍。他把人工超级智能定义为在各方面持续超越大批人类专家，并强调这篇文章是他个人观点，不代表谷歌官方立场。

Tim Genewein asks what happens after artificial general intelligence, laying out four pathways — scaling, paradigm shifts, agent coordination and recursive self-improvement. Effective compute is growing at least 10x a year, he writes, so a $1 billion cognitive workload could cost $1,000 within ten years, and the agent population could grow 1,000x by 2030. He defines artificial superintelligence as consistently outperforming large groups of human experts, and stresses the essay is his own view, not Google's official position.

Meta — MLE Agents：大规模推荐系统中的自主机器学习工程（2026-11-18）

Meta — MLE Agents: autonomous ML engineering in large-scale recommendation systems (2026-11-18)

Meta 的论文介绍 MLE Agents——在生产推荐系统中自主运行机器学习实验的 agent。31 天、131 轮迭代中，agent 运行 1097 次实验，其中 325 次被采纳（29.6%），耗用约 36 万 GPU 小时；加权归一化熵从 0.6611 改善到 0.6388，一次大规模 A/B 测试显示搜索量提升 0.23% ± 0.15。循环由 Claude Opus 4.8 控制器驱动，人类全程在环；论文也坦承 agent 引入了一次延迟回退，提醒我们这个循环尚未完全自主。

Meta's paper on MLE Agents, autonomous agents that run machine-learning experiments in production recommendation systems. Over 31 days and 131 iterations the agents ran 1,097 experiments, 325 of which were accepted (29.6%), using about 360,000 GPU-hours; weighted normalised entropy improved from 0.6611 to 0.6388, and a large A/B test showed search volume up 0.23% ± 0.15. A Claude Opus 4.8 controller runs the loop with humans in the loop; the paper also notes a latency regression the agents introduced, a reminder that the loop is not yet fully autonomous.
