OpenAI 首席科学家提议以困惑度作为衡量 AI 进展的共同标尺（2026-11-22）

OpenAI chief scientist proposes perplexity as a shared yardstick for pacing AI development (2026-11-22)

OpenAI 首席科学家 Jakub Pachocki 发表《Perplexity and Intelligence》，提议前沿实验室采用困惑度——模型预测下一个 token 的能力——作为比较进展和设定共同安全上限的通用指标。他首次披露了 OpenAI 内部的对数困惑度曲线：GPT-3.5 约 0.99、GPT-4 约 0.91、当前模型低于 0.8；低于 0.8 后在网络安全和数学领域出现变革性能力，约 0.76 的模型开始攻克长期悬而未决的数学难题。他预计，随着分数进入 0.68–0.70 区间还会出现进一步的变革性能力，并警告 AI 最早可能在 2027 年开始加速预训练研究本身；他提议各实验室承诺，在监测能力（思维链监测的继任者）赶上之前不越过商定的阈值。CEO Sam Altman 称这是“OpenAI 近期发布的最重要的东西”。微软 AI 首席执行官 Mustafa Suleyman 当天表示支持这一提议。

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published "Perplexity and Intelligence", proposing that frontier labs adopt perplexity — how well a model predicts the next token — as a common metric for comparing progress and setting shared safety limits. He disclosed OpenAI's internal log-perplexity curve for the first time: GPT-3.5 at about 0.99, GPT-4 at about 0.91, current models below 0.8, with transformative capabilities in cybersecurity and mathematics emerging below 0.8 and a model at about 0.76 producing breakthroughs on long-open maths problems. He expects further transformative capabilities as scores enter the 0.68–0.70 window, warns AI could start accelerating pretraining research itself as soon as 2027, and proposes labs commit not to cross agreed thresholds until monitoring — a successor to chain-of-thought monitors — catches up. CEO Sam Altman called it "the most important thing OpenAI has published in awhile". Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman endorsed the proposal the same day.

Z.ai released GLM-5.4, its first flagship built on a Loop Transformer: 28 layers of the roughly 750-billion-parameter GLM-5 base run twice per forward pass, giving a deeper network without adding parameters. Z.ai claims intelligence comparable to 2T–3T parameter models, though it published no flagship benchmark table to back that up — the only quantitative comparison in the release is a 30B ablation. The model adds native vision, a 1M-token context, and a "Generalized Coding" push into video, games, 3D and CAD work. It is live for GLM Coding Plan subscribers in ZCode and Z.ai Chat; API access and open weights are still to come. Z.ai discloses one flaw itself: the model can fall into overly long "marathon thinking" loops.

Thinking Machines 发布 Inkling 2 Mini，20 GB 的循环模型，可在笔记本上运行（2026-11-20）

Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, a 20 GB looped model that runs on a laptop (2026-11-20)

Thinking Machines 发布 Inkling 2 Mini，一个开源权重的循环 Transformer 模型，小到可以在笔记本上运行：总参数 264 亿、激活 34 亿，24 层中的中间两组每 token 运行三次。它在 DeepSWE v1.1（48.2%）和 Vals Index v2.2（46.5%）上领先所有 1000 亿参数以下的开源权重模型，约 20 GB 的体积使其可在 MacBook Pro 上离线运行——团队演示了它在飞行途中几分钟内搭建出一个可玩的飞行模拟器。随模型一同发布的还有一个可变循环研究模型：循环次数增加时，其无思维链时间跨度从 16 秒提升到 37 秒，串行推理分数持续提升到十次循环，而思维链保持可读，智能体破坏测试分数并未上升。团队也坦言：循环提升推理能力，但不增加存储的知识。

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weight looped transformer small enough to run on a laptop: 26.4B total parameters, 3.4B active, with the two middle blocks of its 24 layers running three times per token. It leads open-weight models under 100B parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%), and its ~20 GB footprint lets it run offline on a MacBook Pro — the team demoed it building a playable flight simulator mid-flight. The release includes a variable-loop research model: with more loops its no-CoT time horizon grew from 16 to 37 seconds and serial-reasoning scores kept improving out to ten loops, while its chain of thought stayed legible and it did not trend higher on agentic-sabotage tests. Looping improves reasoning, the team notes, but not stored knowledge.
